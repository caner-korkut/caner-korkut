I lead **[ICTLAB](https://www.ictlab.io)**, an IT consultancy in Brussels. I founded it in 2022. Our team of five helps Belgian and EU organizations with security, compliance and AI. We do penetration tests, ISO 27001 audits, cloud architecture and AI infrastructure. I am a certified **ISO 27001 Lead Auditor** and an experienced penetration tester. I build security and AI systems that go to production.

## 🚀 What I'm building

**[GWARD](https://gward.be)**: a cloud SOC-as-a-Service for European SMEs that cannot justify a full in-house security team. It gives 24/7 threat detection, automated incident response and a NIS2/GDPR compliance dashboard.

**[ICTLAB](https://www.ictlab.io)**: cybersecurity, DevSecOps, cloud and AI consulting for Belgian and EU clients. Our clients work in regulated sectors (finance, healthcare, public sector) or are scale-ups.

## 📚 Open source we maintain

We publish free material for Belgian and EU security teams under [`@ictlab-be`](https://github.com/ictlab-be) (CC BY 4.0):

- **[nis2-readiness-checklist](https://github.com/ictlab-be/nis2-readiness-checklist)**: a self-assessment for the Belgian transposition of NIS2. It covers scope, governance, Article 21 measures, incident reporting and supplier security.
- **[iso27001-control-mapping](https://github.com/ictlab-be/iso27001-control-mapping)**: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A mapped to NIS2 Article 21. It includes coverage and gap notes for dual-compliance programmes.
