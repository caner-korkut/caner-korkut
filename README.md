I lead **[ICTLAB](https://www.ictlab.io)**, a Brussels-based IT consultancy I founded in 2022. Our five-person team helps Belgian and EU organizations stay secure, compliant and AI-ready — from penetration testing and ISO 27001 audits to cloud architecture and applied AI infrastructure. I'm a certified **ISO 27001 Lead Auditor** and an experienced penetration tester, focused on building security and AI systems that actually ship.

## 🚀 What I'm building

**[GWARD](https://gward.be)** — Cloud-native **SOC-as-a-Service**. 24/7 threat detection, automated incident response and a NIS2/GDPR compliance dashboard for European SMEs that can't justify a full in-house security team.

**[ICTLAB](https://www.ictlab.io)** — Cybersecurity, DevSecOps, cloud and AI consulting for Belgian and EU clients across regulated sectors (finance, healthcare, public sector, scale-ups).

## 🤖 On AI

I'm deploying **production RAG and LLM infrastructure** for European clients — and securing it. As models move into critical workflows, my team helps organisations design private inference stacks, threat-model AI agents, run AI red-team exercises against deployed assistants, and integrate AI capabilities into SOC workflows. **Security and AI are converging — we make sure our clients lead, not follow.**

## 📚 Open source we maintain

Under [`@ictlab-be`](https://github.com/ictlab-be) — practical, free-to-reuse artefacts for Belgian and EU security teams (CC BY 4.0):

- **[nis2-readiness-checklist](https://github.com/ictlab-be/nis2-readiness-checklist)** — Self-assessment for the Belgian transposition of NIS2: scope, governance, Article 21 measures, incident reporting and supplier security.
- **[iso27001-control-mapping](https://github.com/ictlab-be/iso27001-control-mapping)** — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A mapped to NIS2 Article 21 with coverage and gap notes for dual-compliance programmes.
